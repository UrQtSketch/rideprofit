# ⚡ RideProfit - Delivery Rider Profit & Petrol Calculator

A mobile-first, high-speed profit calculator built specifically for gig delivery riders (Zomato, Swiggy, Blinkit, Zepto, Porter).

**Created by Deepak**

---

## 🚀 Features

- **Instant Real-Time Calculation:** Deducts fuel/electricity costs automatically as you type.
- **🏍️ Petrol & ⚡ EV Modes:** Switch between petrol bike mileage & electric vehicle charging expenses with 1 tap.
- **⚙️ Saved Vehicle Settings:** Saves petrol rate, mileage, and maintenance reserve locally in the browser (`localStorage`).
- **Khali Wapsi? (Return Trip):** Easily calculate 2x distance for deliveries in empty/dead zones.
- **Service & Tyre Reserve:** Optional toggle for vehicle maintenance reserves.
- **📊 Shift Tracker:** Save orders during a delivery shift to track total payout, total fuel burnt, and net earnings for the day.
- **Mobile-First Ergonomics:** Numeric keypad popup (`inputmode="decimal"`), quick clear buttons, thumb-friendly tap targets (>= 48px), and sunlight high-contrast colors.
- **Offline / PWA Ready:** Installable to mobile home screen via `manifest.json`.
- **Legal & Transparency:** Integrated Terms of Service, Disclaimer, and Privacy modals.
